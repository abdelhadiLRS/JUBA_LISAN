# JUBA LISAN — Brand & Product Direction

## Product promise

**JUBA LISAN** is a personal language-learning environment designed to turn short daily practice into durable language mastery.

Core loop:

> Learn → Practice → Recall → Speak → Master

The product should optimize for retention and confidence, not lesson completion alone.

## Brand principles

1. **Fresh** — modern, bright, energetic and uncluttered.
2. **Human** — the interface should feel encouraging rather than mechanical.
3. **Focused** — every screen should make the next useful action obvious.
4. **Progressive** — progress is visible through skill mastery, retention and consistency.
5. **Global** — Arabic/RTL and international languages are first-class experiences.
6. **Distinctive** — do not imitate Duolingo, Babbel, Busuu or other language-learning brands.

## Visual direction

- Primary visual language: warm amber/gold energy balanced by deep ink neutrals and clean surfaces.
- Use generous whitespace, soft depth, rounded cards and restrained gradients.
- Motion should communicate state and progress, not decoration.
- Prefer small celebratory interactions after meaningful learning actions.
- Keep contrast and keyboard accessibility at production quality.

## Experience pillars

### Daily Momentum

The home experience answers three questions immediately:

- What should I do now?
- What is due for review?
- How close am I to today's goal?

### Durable Memory

Learning surfaces should expose recall, review and spaced repetition as first-class actions.

### Real Communication

Speaking, listening and AI conversation are learning activities. They should feed mistakes and weak skills back into the learning loop.

### Visible Mastery

Progress should be represented by skill areas such as vocabulary, grammar, listening, reading, speaking, pronunciation and retention rather than XP alone.

## Naming

Use **JUBA LISAN** as the canonical product name in user-facing copy, metadata, page titles and navigation.

Use **JUBA AI Tutor** for the conversational tutor where a named assistant is useful.

Avoid introducing the previous product name in new UI, documentation or marketing copy.
